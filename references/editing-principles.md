# Editing Principles

## Story first

Editing should communicate the story before showing technical skill.

## Cut with purpose

A cut should be motivated by:

- information
- action
- emotion
- rhythm
- continuity
- attention

## Pacing

Pacing should change with the content.

Fast does not automatically mean engaging.

Slow does not automatically mean boring.

## Visual variety

Change visual information when it benefits comprehension or retention, not simply because a fixed time interval has passed.

## Restraint

Professional editing often means knowing what not to add.
