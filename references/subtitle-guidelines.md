# Subtitle Guidelines

## Goals

Subtitles must be:

- accurate
- synchronized
- readable
- correctly positioned
- language-aware

## Persian / RTL

Use:

- correct RTL layout
- proper Persian punctuation
- correct Persian glyph shaping
- sensible line breaks
- careful handling of Latin words and numbers

## Animation

Prefer subtle animation:

- fade
- slide
- phrase reveal
- emphasis
- scale

Do not animate every word unless the requested style specifically requires it.

## Font

Doran Fanum is a suitable Persian subtitle choice when installed. Otherwise use an appropriate readable fallback.
