# Sound Design

## Priority

1. Dialogue
2. Important action SFX
3. Ambience
4. Music

## SFX

Use sound effects to reinforce actual visual or narrative events.

Examples:

- impact
- whoosh
- riser
- drop
- click
- UI interaction
- environmental sound

## Music

Music should support the emotional direction of the video.

Automatically reduce music under dialogue.

Avoid music that competes with speech.
