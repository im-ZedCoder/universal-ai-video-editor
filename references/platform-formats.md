# Platform Formats

## Vertical short-form

Typical:

- 1080 × 1920
- 9:16

Useful for:

- Instagram Reels
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts

## Landscape

Typical:

- 1920 × 1080
- 16:9

Useful for:

- YouTube
- presentations
- websites

## Square

Typical:

- 1080 × 1080
- 1:1

Use when the destination requires or benefits from square composition.

Always preserve safe areas for platform UI.
